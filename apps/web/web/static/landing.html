How an Order Flows

From the first word to the kitchen ticket, every step is validated and confirmed.

1
🗣️
Customer Speaks
Voice or text — the customer requests items in natural language.
2
🧠
AI Interprets
The assistant parses the request and maps it to your actual menu.
3
✅
Menu Validates
Sizes, toppings, availability, and prices are checked against the source of truth.
4
📋
Customer Confirms
The full cart and total are read back before the customer approves.
5
🎫
Kitchen Receives
A kitchen ticket is created and the order enters the KDS queue.

Built for Real Restaurants

Every feature below is implemented and working in this codebase — no mockups, no vaporware.

🎙️
Voice Ordering
Real-time speech-to-text via Deepgram, processed by a Groq LLM agent with Silero VAD, streamed over LiveKit WebRTC.
💬
Text Chat
Groq-powered text ordering with keyword fallback. Customers type orders as natural sentences.
📦
Menu Validation Engine
Every item, size, and topping is validated against the live menu JSON. Out-of-stock items are rejected in real time.
🧾
Kitchen Display
Orders flow into a Kanban-style KDS with Queued → Preparing → Ready stages. Staff advance tickets with one click.
🔒
Role-Based Auth
PBKDF2 password hashing, HMAC-signed tokens, IP rate limiting, three-tier access (public, staff, admin).
💳
Payments & POS
COD, UPI, and Razorpay with mock fallback. POS sync ready for Petpooja and Posist integrations.
